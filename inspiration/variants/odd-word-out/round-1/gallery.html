Odd Word Out

Phone screen mockups for the imposter word game. 3 designers ran (opus (Claude Opus 5.5), fable (Claude Fable 5.1), sonnet (Claude Sonnet 5.5)), 6 variants, 6 screens each, all at phone size (390 × 844). Every screen uses the same sample round so you can compare like for like.

Refero's live search wasn't available, so each designer worked from named reference apps instead.

Roles · new every round

  • Crew see the secret word and their own angle, e.g. "give a clue about a sound".
  • Imposter sees only the category, one fuzzy hint ("It's tall") and an angle.
  • Echo gets a nearby word (Windmill instead of Lighthouse) and doesn't know it. Innocent, but looks guilty.
  • Optional: Jester wants to be voted out. Detective learns one person who is definitely innocent.

A round · about 3 minutes

  1. Secret card. Hold to peek at your role.
  2. Clue. Type one word that fits your angle. Everyone's clues show up at once.
  3. Talk. 60 to 90 seconds, out loud or on the call.
  4. Vote on the app. Hidden until everyone votes.
  5. Last chance. A caught imposter can guess the word to escape.

Hearts · everyone starts with 3

  • You voted for an innocent player: you lose 1.
  • You voted for the imposter: safe.
  • Imposter caught: imposter loses 1. Escaped: imposter gains 1.
  • Only wrong voters lose, 1 each, so nobody is knocked out in one round.
  • 0 hearts makes you a Ghost: you still give clues but can't vote. Most hearts after 5 rounds wins.
