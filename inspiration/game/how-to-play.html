Odd Word Out · How to play

How to play

A word game where one player is secretly the imposter. Everyone plays on their own phone, in the same room or on a call. A round takes about 3 minutes.

01

Start

Everyone opens the site on their own phone. One person creates a room and becomes the host. Everyone else joins with the 4-letter code.

02

Lobby

Wait here until everyone is in. Only the host can change settings and start. + Bot adds a computer player, which is handy for testing on your own.

03

Secret card

Everyone gets a secret card. The screen is black for everyone, so nobody can guess your role from your screen colour. Hold the box to read your card, and let go to hide it. Then tap Ready.

There are three possible cards:

  • Crew (green): the secret word.
  • Imposter (red): no word, just a hint.
  • Echo: a nearby word. Their card looks exactly like crew, so they don't know they're the Echo.
04

Talk

Go round in the order shown. Each person says one word out loud that fits their angle. Nothing is typed, so it works in the same room or on a call. Then argue about who sounded off.

Your card is gone now, so you have to remember your word.

05

Vote

Tap who you think the imposter is, then tap Vote. Nobody sees who voted for whom until the end. The player with the most votes is out. A tie means nobody is out.

06

Last chance

This only happens if the imposter gets the most votes. They get one guess at the secret word. If they're right, they escape.

07

Result

Everything is revealed and hearts change. The host starts the next round, and everyone gets new roles and a new word.

08

Hearts & Ghosts

  • Everyone starts with 3 hearts.
  • You voted for an innocent player (including the Echo): you lose 1.
  • You voted for the imposter: safe.
  • Imposter caught and guessed wrong: imposter loses 1.
  • Imposter escaped or guessed the word: imposter gets 1 back, up to 3.
  • 0 hearts makes you a Ghost. Ghosts still get a word and say clues, but can't vote and can't be the imposter.
09

Game over

The game ends after the last round, or earlier if only 2 players still have hearts. The winner has the most hearts. If it's a tie, whoever voted correctly most often wins.
