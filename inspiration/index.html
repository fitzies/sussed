Who's Lying?

Four ideas for a phone party game. Everyone joins from their own phone with a room code. Somebody is secretly working against the group. Random luck keeps every round different.

One of them is lying. Tap who you think it is.

    Idea 01My pick

    Castle of Liars

    9 Kings meets Among Us

    Everyone builds one shared castle on a 3×3 grid and has to survive the monster nights. One or two players are secretly Traitors.

    1. DayYou get 3 random cards. Pick one and put it face-down on a square.
    2. NightMonsters attack. Every card flips over and you watch the fight together.
    3. VoteSomebody's card was cursed. Everyone says what they placed. Vote someone into the dungeon.

    The traitor: their cards look normal until night. Then the archers shoot the wrong way and the wall crumbles. They have to lie about which card was theirs.

    Idea 02

    Witch's Brew

    The simplest one. 10-minute games.

    The group brews potions in one shared cauldron. Each round has a recipe like "make it RED and HOT".

    1. PickYou get 3 random ingredients. Drop one in. Nobody sees which.
    2. RevealThe cauldron shows what went in, shuffled. Then it works, fizzles or explodes.
    3. VoteSomebody put a Rotten Egg in. Who was it?

    The saboteur: wants the potion to blow up. Chaos Mushrooms add a random roll, so a bad potion could be bad luck or a liar.

    Try a round

    You + Gran, Dad and Mia

    Your ingredients: pick one to drop in

    Idea 03

    Odd Word Out

    The Imposter game you know, plus loot

    The group goes through a dungeon room by room. Every room is one quick round of Imposter.

    1. WordEveryone gets the same secret word. The imposter only gets "???".
    2. ClueEveryone says one word about it. The imposter has to bluff.
    3. LootCatch them and you open a chest with a random power. Miss and you lose a heart.

    The imposter: new one every room. Loot like "Peek at someone's word" or "Double vote" makes each room feel different.

      Idea 04

      Horde Night

      Megabonk with a monster on the team

      Everyone runs around one map. Your hero attacks on its own. You move with one thumb and grab gems. When you level up you pick 1 of 3 random upgrades.

      1. FightSlimes pour in from every side. Your swords spin on their own.
      2. LevelChoose an upgrade. More swords, bigger magnet, faster boots.
      3. VoteAfter each wave, vote on who the Mimic is.

      The Mimic: plays like everyone else, but their upgrades secretly curse the horde: giant slimes, faster slimes, more slimes.
